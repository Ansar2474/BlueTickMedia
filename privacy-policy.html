Bluetick Media LLC (“Bluetick Media”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates this website and provides SEO, paid advertising, social media marketing, content creation and web development services. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our website.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you fill in a form, call us, message us or ask for a quote or audit, you may give us:

  • Your full name, phone or WhatsApp number and email address
  • Your company name and industry
  • The services you are interested in
  • Any message or project details you choose to share

Information collected automatically

  • Your IP address and approximate location
  • Your browser and device type
  • The pages you visit, the time you spend on them and how you found us
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

Information from third parties

We may receive information from Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) or LinkedIn when you interact with our pages, ad campaigns or booking tools. That information is also governed by those platforms’ own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide quotes, audits or consultations
  • Deliver, maintain and improve our services
  • Send you updates, proposals and marketing messages, which you can opt out of at any time
  • Analyse website traffic and campaign performance
  • Meet our legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following grounds:

  • Consent, for example when you submit a contact form
  • Contractual necessity, where we need your data to provide services you have asked for
  • Legitimate interest, in improving our website and marketing our services
  • Legal compliance, including under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (UAE PDPL)

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our website, and measure the performance of our Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns. The tools we may use include:

  • Google Analytics, to analyse traffic and behaviour
  • Google Ads and Meta Pixel, to measure and optimise campaigns
  • Other marketing or CRM tools, to manage leads and communications

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling them may affect some features of the website.

5. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers such as hosting, email, CRM and analytics providers, who are bound by confidentiality
  • Advertising partners such as Google and Meta, in aggregated or pseudonymised form where possible
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, when required by law or to protect legal rights
  • A successor business, in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing without your consent.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, to meet legal or contractual obligations, and to resolve disputes. After that, we delete or anonymise it securely.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may:

  • Ask to access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data
  • Withdraw your consent to marketing at any time
  • Object to or ask us to restrict certain processing

To make a request, email hello@bluetickmedia.ae.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and individuals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed outside the UAE, for example through cloud hosting or third-party platforms. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data receives an equivalent level of protection.

11. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to external websites and social platforms. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies separately.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows the latest revision. By continuing to use the website after a change, you accept the updated policy.
